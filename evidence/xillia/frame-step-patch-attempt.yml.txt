    Notes: "Use with the community 60 FPS patch. The game advances its world by one 60 Hz tick per frame (frame step at 0x23f12c = ticks x 1.0, stored in the frame-timing object +0xa4), so above 60 FPS it runs fast. This scales that step by 60/fps from the word at 0xf11ac8, which the VR profile sets to 1/fps every frame (game_frame_time_f32). With the word at 0 (VR off) the step is unchanged."
